ABOUT THIS EBOOK

What You'll Explore

Before You Go to Heaven takes the foundational research behind Genesis Engineered Interpretation and focuses it on one of the most discussed topics in biblical study: heaven itself.

Drawing on the GEI framework — the same independent research that produced the Adam A-DAM hydraulic hypothesis — this ebook examines what Genesis 1–6 may actually describe when it refers to the heavens, the firmament, and the waters above.

Built on published research. The GEI framework behind this ebook has been published on SSRN and Zenodo with permanent DOI identifiers. This is independent, transparent, open-access research.

Key topics include:

  • What does "heaven" mean within the hydraulic reading of Genesis?
  • How does the firmament function as an engineered structure?
  • The relationship between the waters above, the waters below, and the system in between
  • How the Adam A-DAM hypothesis connects to the broader question of heaven
  • GEI's interpretive method: engineering language applied to ancient text
THE AUTHOR

Wilbert Bouie Jr.

Wilbert Bouie Jr. is an independent researcher and the creator of Genesis Engineered Interpretation (GEI). His work proposes a hydraulic and engineering-based reading of Genesis 1–6, examining Adam as a dam, the firmament as a partition, and the six days as stages of a water-control system.

His research is published on SSRN and Zenodo, and he maintains an ORCID profile for research attribution.

THE GEI FRAMEWORK

Genesis Engineered Interpretation

GEI is an independent research framework that reads Genesis 1–6 through the lens of water systems, hydraulic engineering, and figurative language. The core hypothesis — that Adam functions as a dam within an engineered water system — forms the backbone of this ebook's exploration of heaven.
