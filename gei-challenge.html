HOW TO QUALIFY

Evidence beats opinion.

  1. Identify one specific GEI statement or interpretation.
  2. Quote or precisely reference the relevant Genesis passage, language, or engineering claim.
  3. Provide reliable, checkable evidence that contradicts the claim.
  4. Explain why the contradiction materially weakens or disproves that part of GEI.
  5. Address the strongest reasonable response to your argument.

Disagreement, ridicule, unsupported assertions, and arguments that merely offer another interpretation do not qualify by themselves.

INDEPENDENT REVIEW

Who decides whether GEI has been disproved?

A verified challenge must be assessed by an independent reviewer who is not Wilbert Bouie Jr. and is not the person submitting the challenge. The reviewer should have relevant expertise in at least one area implicated by the entry, such as biblical studies, Hebrew or ancient-language research, ancient Near Eastern history, hydrology, or engineering.

  • Independence: the reviewer must disclose relevant financial, professional, personal, or research conflicts and recuse themselves where impartiality could reasonably be questioned.
  • Evidence-first decision: the reviewer evaluates the exact GEI claim, the submitter's sources, the reasoning, and any written response from Wilbert.
  • Public explanation: every verified or rejected determination receives a short written rationale with sources, the specific claim examined, and the review date.
  • Meaningful scope: a finding applies to the specific claim actually refuted. It does not automatically disprove every part of GEI.
  • Scoreboard integrity: only after the written determination is published is the issue title marked [GEI CHALLENGE] [VERIFIED] and counted as a verified challenge.

Reviewer appointment: no reviewer is being represented as appointed yet. The reviewer’s name, relevant qualifications, independence statement, and any disclosed conflicts must be published on this page before the prize contest officially opens.

HOW THE SCOREBOARD CHANGES

Submission is not the same as proof.

  • Submission: an entry is received and listed publicly. This does not affect the streak.
  • Under review: the argument is being examined against the published criteria.
  • Verified challenge: the independent reviewer publishes a reasoned finding that the evidence materially refutes a defined GEI claim.
  • Streak status: the streak remains active until a verified challenge is recorded. A verified refutation changes the scoreboard to “STREAK STOPPED” and the finding is published.

The scoreboard will count only public GitHub issues with titles beginning [GEI CHALLENGE]. A verified result must be explicitly marked [GEI CHALLENGE] [VERIFIED] after the independent decision is published.
